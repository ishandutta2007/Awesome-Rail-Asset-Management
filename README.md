# Awesome-Rail-Asset-Management

## Top Rail Asset Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Railway EAM, Rolling Stock & Infrastructure Maintenance, Condition-Based Maintenance, Work Orders, Linear Assets & Fleet Reliability*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS/commercial platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Rail Asset Management**. These systems help rail operators and infrastructure managers maintain rolling stock, tracks, signaling, and fixed assets through work-order management, preventive and condition-based maintenance, asset hierarchies, compliance tracking, and performance analytics.



**Examples** include Hitachi Rail (HMAX and related), HxGN EAM, IBM Maximo, IFS Cloud, Ellis Rail, RailCube, Trapeze Rail, AssetWise Rail, RailSmart, and Assetic (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise rail asset management platforms with deep linear-asset modeling, regulatory compliance, and integrated APM are predominantly commercial. Open-source activity includes general CMMS/EAM tools that can be adapted, condition-based maintenance decision systems for rail, infrastructure asset management frameworks, and emerging rail-specific projects. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[IBM Maximo](https://www.ibm.com/products/maximo)**  

  Leading enterprise asset management platform widely used in rail and transit for work management, asset performance management (APM), IoT integration, and compliance-heavy operations.



- **[HxGN EAM (Hexagon)](https://hexagon.com/)**  

  Enterprise asset management solution strong in asset hierarchies, preventive maintenance, work planning, and asset investment planning for transportation and rail networks.



- **[IFS Cloud](https://www.ifs.com/)**  

  Unified EAM, field service, and supply-chain platform well suited to transportation operators managing rolling stock, depots, and linear infrastructure.



- **[Hitachi Rail (HMAX and digital asset solutions)](https://www.hitachirail.com/)**  

  Rail-specific digital asset management and AI-enhanced solutions covering trains, signaling, and infrastructure optimization.



- **[Trapeze Rail / related EAM](https://www.trapezegroup.com/)**  

  Transit and rail-focused solutions that include asset and maintenance management capabilities for operators.



- **[RailCube, Ellis Rail, RailSmart](https://www.railcube.com/)**  

  Specialized rail and transit asset, maintenance, and operations platforms serving various regional and operational needs.



- **[AssetWise (Bentley), Assetic](https://www.bentley.com/)**  

  Infrastructure and asset management platforms used for rail linear assets, lifecycle planning, and capital decision support.



- **[Other rail & transportation EAM platforms](https://www.ibm.com/products/maximo)**  

  Additional commercial systems covering predictive maintenance, depot management, and network asset performance.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OpenSourceRail](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail)**  

  Open-source stack for urban rail design, simulation, operations, QA, maintenance, and certification-evidence support, aimed at light metro and locally manufactured systems.



- **[Railway CBM / condition-based maintenance decision systems](https://github.com/search?q=railway+CBM+OR+ferroviario+mantenimiento+OR+rail+condition+based)**  

  Reproducible decision-support projects that prioritize rail maintenance, sequence workshop interventions, and evaluate trade-offs using condition data.



- **[General open-source CMMS / EAM](https://github.com/search?q=CMMS+OR+EAM+open+source)**  

  Computerized maintenance management systems that provide work orders, asset registers, preventive maintenance scheduling, and inventory—adaptable to rail workshops and fleets.



- **[GIAMS and infrastructure asset management frameworks](https://github.com/search?q=infrastructure+asset+management+OR+GIAMS)**  

  Open-source platforms for general infrastructure asset management that can be extended to bridges, track, and other linear rail assets.



- **[Maintenance optimization & reliability libraries](https://github.com/search?q=maintenance+optimization+OR+reliability+engineering+OR+CBM)**  

  Python and OR-tools based projects for prioritization, spare-parts planning, and condition-based maintenance logic.



- **[Linear asset & GIS helpers](https://github.com/search?q=linear+asset+OR+railway+GIS+OR+track+management)**  

  Tools for modeling linear assets, track segments, and spatial relationships useful in rail infrastructure management.



- **[IoT & condition monitoring pipelines](https://github.com/search?q=condition+monitoring+OR+predictive+maintenance+rail)**  

  Open data pipelines and analytics notebooks that ingest sensor data for rail asset health scoring.



- **[Other rail operations & maintenance experiments](https://github.com/search?q=rail+maintenance+OR+rolling+stock+OR+depot+management)**  

  Community and research projects exploring fleet reliability, workshop scheduling, and digital twins for rail.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **ERPNext / Odoo maintenance modules**: General open-source ERP systems with asset and maintenance capabilities that some operators customize.

- **Time-series & anomaly detection**: Libraries for analyzing vibration, temperature, or usage data from rolling stock and infrastructure.

- **Work-order mobile apps**: Open frameworks for field technicians to receive and close maintenance tasks.

- **Documentation & standards tracking**: Tools for managing maintenance procedures, safety cases, and compliance evidence.

- **Simulation & digital twin foundations**: Open simulation environments that can support what-if maintenance planning.

- Spreadsheet + script hybrids still used for smaller fleets or pilot condition-monitoring projects.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Start with a solid open-source **CMMS/EAM** core for work orders and asset registers, add **condition-based decision logic** from rail-specific research projects, and layer GIS/linear-asset modeling for infrastructure.  

**OpenSourceRail** and similar efforts provide broader open stacks for certain urban rail contexts.  

Commercial platforms (IBM Maximo, HxGN EAM, IFS Cloud, Hitachi Rail solutions, Trapeze, etc.) deliver rail-specific data models, regulatory support, large-scale APM, and vendor ecosystems that most open-source assemblies require significant domain engineering to approach.  

Many operators run hybrid environments—open analytics and CBM tools feeding into enterprise EAM systems of record.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Rail asset management systems support safety-critical infrastructure and rolling stock. Data integrity, audit trails, configuration control, and compliance with railway safety regulations are essential.

- Open-source tools can reduce licensing cost and increase transparency but require deep domain expertise, rigorous validation, cybersecurity measures, and ongoing maintenance. They are not automatic substitutes for certified or widely proven commercial EAM platforms in operational rail environments. Operators remain fully responsible for safety and regulatory compliance.
